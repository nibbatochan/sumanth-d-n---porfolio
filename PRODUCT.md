## Product Purpose

A digital portfolio showcasing Sumanth D N's work in UI/UX Product Design, Design Systems, Visual Identity, and Creative Tech. The product exists to demonstrate high-level design execution, tactile craft, and technical creativity to secure full-time design roles and select freelance projects.

## Positioning

"Designing warm, minimal digital products & visual identities." 
Bridges tactile, retro-futuristic creative technology (interactive 3D ASCII flight physics, laser particle mechanics) with human-scale, production-ready product UI/UX craftsmanship.

## Operating Context

- Viewed on desktop (large screens, standard 1080p, laptops) and mobile browsers.
- High-performance SPA with client-side routing, instant loading, dark/light theme switching, and interactive audio feedback.
- Production hosting on Vercel with automated deployment pipeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Tech Stack**: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Phosphor Icons, HTML5 Canvas, Canvas Confetti.
- **Interactive 3D Dove Engine**: Real-time ASCII rasterization with cursor-tracking flight physics, target sight reticle, and laser projectile dodging.
- **Modals**: Full-screen interactive case study walkthroughs and quick-contact drawer.
- **Preloader**: 1.5s cubic-eased initial loading sequence with synchronized status logs.
- **Audio System**: Web Audio synthetic tactile SFX for clicks, pops, laser whooshes, and completions.
- **Responsive**: Adaptive desktop pill navigation and mobile slide-down hamburger drawer.

## Product Principles

1. **Tactile & Human**: Every interaction responds with deliberate motion, weight, and optional sound.
2. **Artifact-First (Experience Mode)**: The portfolio showcases work immediately from the hero viewport without unnecessary friction.
3. **Warm Minimalism**: High density of craft with zero clutter — warm amber/terracotta lighting over structured obsidian geometry.
4. **Performance & Crash-Immunity**: Fast 60fps canvas animations with robust fallbacks and mobile responsiveness.
