# NoBroker Case Study: Illustration & Visual Asset Guide

This document outlines **8 high-impact visual scenarios** designed for the NoBroker case study page (`/#/project/nobroker`)..

---

## 🎨 Global Art Direction & Design System Alignment

To ensure visual consistency with Sumanth's editorial portfolio and NoBroker's brand identity, all illustrations and renders should adhere to the following visual rules:

* **Color Palette**:
  * Base Backgrounds: Deep Slate/Charcoal (`#09090B`, `#121215`) or Crisp Editorial White (`#FAFAFA`)
  * Brand Accent: Vibrant Orange (`#F97316`, `#EA580C`)

* **Visual Philosophy**:
  * **No Generic AI Slop**: Avoid generic corporate 2D clip art, floating faceless pastel characters, or cluttered abstract shapes.
  * **Technical High-Craft**: Prefer clean 3D isometric clay/glassmorphic renders, technical architectural diagrams, Apple/Linear style device mockups, and comparative split-screen schematics.
  * **Legibility First**: Visuals must serve as functional diagrams explaining complex rental mechanics (commute bottlenecks, wide-angle distortion, alert fatigue) at a glance.

### Scenario 8: Automated Crowd-Sourced Delisting Loop
* **Page Placement**: Section 07 (`#gaps-section`), anchoring the "Reflections, Honest Gaps & Next Steps" roadmap.
* **UX Objective**: Explains the technical architecture of the automated post-call verification loop that archives ghost listings within 24 hours.
* **Visual Style**: Technical circular state machine / feedback loop architecture diagram.
* **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9`.
* **Key Elements**:
  * Step 1: Tenant calls owner via in-app masked dialer.
  * Step 2: Automated post-call micro-prompt: "Was this flat still available?".
  * Step 3: Two verified negative signals recorded in database.
  * Step 4: Listing instantly tagged "Delisted: Verified Leased" and removed from search feed.
