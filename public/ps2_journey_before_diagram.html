Insight 01

Distance is not Duration

Railway gates, bottlenecks, and flyovers cause two properties equidistant from an office to have completely different commute realities. Listing distance without rush-hour transit forces renters off the platform.

Insight 02

The Repeat Tab Penalty

Renters don't mind checking Maps once. What breaks the experience is doing it 20 to 30 times across a 2-month search. The research effort compounds until the session is abandoned.

Insight 03

Where the Funnel Leaks

Renters don't leave because they ran out of houses. They leave because investigating basic livability takes more mental energy than browsing the catalogue. Bringing transit data into the feed keeps renters in-app.
